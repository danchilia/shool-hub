## KEY FEATURES

### 1. FEES MANAGEMENT (No more lost fee records)

- Track every shilling collected - who paid, when, how much, method used
- Support for **Cash, M-Pesa, Cheque, Bank Transfer** payments
- Automatic **fee balance calculation** per student
- **Partial payments** - parents can pay in installments, system tracks the balance
- Set different fee structures for different classes (Primary vs Secondary)
- **Late payment fines** - automatic penalty calculation when fees are overdue
- **Fee reminders** - automatic SMS to parents before and after due dates
- **Receipts** - printable payment receipts for every transaction
- **Due fees report** - instantly see all students with unpaid balances
- No more manual fee registers or lost payment records

### 2. CBC & 8-4-4 ASSESSMENT (Kenya curriculum ready)

- **CBC grading** for PP1 to Grade 9:
  - Competency levels: EE, ME, AE, BE (not marks)
  - Learning areas with strands
  - Behaviour assessment (Social, Spiritual, Emotional, Physical, Creative)
  - Colour-coded CBC report cards
- **Traditional grading** for Form 1-4:
  - CAT 1, CAT 2, End Term marks entry
  - Automatic grade calculation (A to E, KCSE scale)
  - GPA, ranking, pass/fail
  - Printable report cards
- **Tabulation sheets** - class-wide comparison of all students

### 3. STUDENT MANAGEMENT (Every student tracked)

- Complete student records: personal details, UPI/NEMIS number, guardian info
- **Admission** - single student or bulk import via CSV file
- **Teacher admission requests** - teachers submit, admin approves
- Student **ID card generation** with QR code
- Student **promotion** - move students to next class at end of year
- Track student history across all years (marks, fees, attendance)
- Student and parent **login portals** to check progress

### 4. ATTENDANCE (Know who is in school)

- Daily student attendance: **Present, Absent, Late**
- Staff attendance tracking
- Exam attendance
- **Monthly/yearly attendance reports** with percentages
- Parents can check their child's attendance from their phone
- Helps identify students with chronic absenteeism

### 5. COMMUNICATION (Reach every parent instantly)

- **Bulk SMS** to all parents or specific classes
- **Email notifications** for account creation, results, payments
- **Africa's Talking SMS** integration (cheapest for Kenya)
- Automatic SMS triggers:
  - Student admitted → SMS to parent
  - Fee paid → confirmation SMS
  - Fee overdue → reminder SMS
  - Exam results published → notification SMS
  - Homework assigned → alert SMS
- **Internal messaging** between staff, or staff to parents

### 6. PAYROLL & HR (Staff management made easy)

- Complete employee records with qualifications, departments, designations
- **Salary templates** with Kenyan deductions:
  - NHIF
  - NSSF
  - PAYE
  - House and Transport allowances
- **Monthly payslip generation** with full breakdown
- **Leave management** - staff apply, admin approves
  - Annual, Sick, Maternity, Compassionate leave
  - Track leave days used vs remaining
- **Advance salary** requests and approvals
- Staff **awards** and recognition

### 7. ACCOUNTING (Every coin accounted for)

- Create bank accounts and petty cash records
- Record all **income** (fees, grants, donations)
- Record all **expenses** (bills, supplies, maintenance, fuel)
- **Transaction ledger** - complete audit trail
- Income vs Expense reports
- Voucher heads for categorizing transactions
- Fee collection automatically links to accounting

### 8. TIMETABLE & HOMEWORK (Organized learning)

- **Class timetable** - set weekly schedule per class
  - Subject, teacher, time, classroom
  - Visible to students and parents
- **Exam timetable** - schedule exams with date, time, hall
- **Homework management**:
  - Teachers create and publish assignments
  - Set due dates
  - Grade/evaluate submissions
  - Parents see homework on their portal

### 9. LIBRARY MANAGEMENT (Track every book)

- Book catalog with title, author, ISBN, category, stock count
- **Issue and return** books to students and staff
- Track overdue books
- Automatic **late fine** calculation
- Students can **request books** from their portal
- Categories: Textbooks, Fiction, Reference, Kiswahili Literature

### 10. TRANSPORT MANAGEMENT (Safe school travel)

- Create bus **routes** with start and end points
- Register **vehicles** with capacity, driver details, insurance info
- Set **pickup points** with scheduled times and fares
- Assign vehicles to routes
- Parents see their child's bus route, stop, and driver contact

### 11. HOSTEL / BOARDING (For boarding schools)

- Hostel buildings with categories (Boys, Girls)
- Room management with bed count and fees
- Student room allocation
- Hostel fee tracking integrated with fee system

### 12. EVENTS & CALENDAR (Keep everyone informed)

- Create school events: Sports Day, Cultural Week, Parents Meeting
- Events appear on everyone's calendar
- Different event types: Sports, Cultural, Academic, Meetings
- Visible to all staff, parents, and students

### 13. REPORTS (Data-driven decisions)

- **Fee reports** - who paid, who owes, daily/weekly/monthly collections
- **Attendance reports** - student and staff attendance percentages
- **Exam reports** - report cards, tabulation sheets, class rankings
- **Financial reports** - income, expenses, balance sheets
- **Payroll reports** - salary summaries, deduction totals
- **Leave reports** - staff leave usage
- All reports **printable** and **exportable**

## FAQ (Common Questions from Schools)

**Q: Do we need internet?**
A: Yes, the system runs in a web browser. Any internet connection works (even phone hotspot).

**Q: Can parents use it on their phone?**
A: Yes, it works on any phone with a browser. No app download needed.

**Q: What if internet goes down?**
A: Your data is safe on the server. Just continue when internet comes back. For critical days (exam entry, fee collection), a simple phone hotspot is enough.

**Q: Can we use it for both primary and secondary?**
A: Yes, one system handles PP1 through Form 4, both CBC and 8-4-4.

**Q: What about our old student records?**
A: We can enter your existing student data during setup. Historical marks from previous years can be entered if needed.

**Q: Is our data safe?**
A: Yes, data is stored securely with daily backups. Each school can only see their own data.

**Q: Can we try it first?**
A: Yes, we offer a free demo with sample data so you can see how it works before committing.

**Q: What if a teacher can't use computers?**
A: The system is very simple - if they can use WhatsApp, they can use this. We provide training.

**Q: Do you support M-Pesa payment?**
A: Yes, parents can pay fees via M-Pesa Paybill. The payment is recorded automatically.

**Q: Can multiple schools use one system?**
A: Yes, that's what makes DCK Solutions unique. One platform, multiple schools, each with their own separate data.
