Core Capabilities

Everything a school needs.
Nothing it doesn't.

School Hub by DCK is built from the ground up around how Kenyan schools actually operate. Every module reflects real classroom, bursar, and administration workflows — designed for the way your school runs every day.

Multi-Branch Management

Run one school or fifty from a single login. Each branch is fully isolated — own students, fees, staff, and settings — while the superadmin sees everything from a central dashboard.

Fees & M-Pesa Payments

Issue fee invoices, track balances, and collect payments via M-Pesa STK Push — parents pay from their phone and the system confirms automatically. No manual reconciliation.

CBC & Standard Exams

Kenya's CBC curriculum is a first-class citizen: Learning Areas, Strands, Behaviour Assessments, and CBC Report Cards — alongside the full traditional marks-based exam engine.

Biometric Attendance

Connect fingerprint or card readers to the system via a live API. Scans are pushed to School Hub by DCK in real time and converted to attendance records — no manual data entry.

GPS Bus Tracking

Live map showing every school bus in real time. GPS devices push location data directly to the system. Parents know where the bus is. The transport office has full visibility.

Analytics & At-Risk Students

A composite risk engine scores every student daily on attendance, academic performance, and fee default. The top at-risk students surface automatically — before they fall through the cracks.

Payments

M-Pesa payments that just work.

Parents tap a button, enter their M-Pesa PIN, and the fee is cleared. No PayBill confusion, no staff chasing payments, no manual receipts.
