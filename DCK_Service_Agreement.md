## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS

1.1 **"System"** means the DCK Solutions School Management System, a web-based software platform for managing school operations including but not limited to student records, fee management, academic assessments, attendance, payroll, and communication.

1.2 **"Subscription"** means the periodic payment made by The School to DCK Solutions for continued access to and use of the System.

1.3 **"Data"** means all information entered into the System by The School including but not limited to student records, parent details, staff information, financial records, academic results, and attendance records.

1.4 **"Equipment"** means any computer hardware provided by DCK Solutions to The School under the yearly subscription offer.

1.5 **"Users"** means all persons authorized by The School to access and use the System, including administrators, teachers, accountants, librarians, parents, and students.

1.6 **"Branch"** means The School's dedicated instance on the System with its own data, settings, and user accounts.

2.1 DCK Solutions agrees to provide The School with:

   (a) Access to the DCK Solutions School Management System

   (b) Initial system setup and configuration including:

## ARTICLE 4: GRACE PERIOD AND SERVICE SUSPENSION

4.1 The System will display a subscription expiry warning to The School **fourteen (14) days** before the subscription end date.

4.2 Upon subscription expiry, The School shall have a **grace period of seven (7) days** to make payment.

4.3 If payment is not received within the grace period, DCK Solutions reserves the right to **suspend access** to the System. During suspension:
   - The School's data shall be **preserved** and not deleted
   - Users will only be able to access the dashboard (read-only)
   - All other features will be disabled
   - Data entry and modifications will not be possible

4.4 Service shall be **restored within 4 hours** of payment confirmation.

4.5 If the subscription remains unpaid for more than **ninety (90) days** after the expiry date, DCK Solutions reserves the right to:
   - Archive The School's data
   - Deallocate system resources
   - Terminate this Agreement under Article 11

4.6 DCK Solutions shall provide The School with written notice via email and SMS before any data archival or deletion.

## ARTICLE 5: DATA OWNERSHIP AND PROTECTION

5.1 **Data Ownership:** All data entered into the System by The School, including student records, financial records, staff records, and academic results, remains the **sole property of The School**.

5.2 **Data Protection:** DCK Solutions shall handle all data in compliance with the **Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019** and shall:

   (a) Process personal data only for the purposes specified in this Agreement

   (b) Implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect personal data against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction

   (c) Not share, sell, or transfer The School's data to any third party without The School's written consent

   (d) Ensure that all employees and contractors who have access to personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations

   (e) Notify The School within **48 hours** of becoming aware of any data breach affecting The School's data

5.3 **Data Backup:** DCK Solutions shall maintain daily backups of all data. Backups shall be stored securely and retained for a minimum of **thirty (30) days**.

5.4 **Data Export:** The School has the right to request a complete export of their data in a standard format (CSV/SQL) at any time. DCK Solutions shall fulfill such requests within **five (5) business days**.

5.5 **Data Deletion:** Upon termination of this Agreement (and after the retention period in Article 11.4), DCK Solutions shall permanently delete The School's data upon written request and provide written confirmation of deletion.

5.6 **Data Access:** Only authorized personnel of DCK Solutions shall have access to The School's data, and only for the purposes of system maintenance, support, and backup.

---

## ARTICLE 6: DATA SECURITY MEASURES

6.1 DCK Solutions shall implement and maintain the following security measures:

   (a) **Encryption:** All data transmitted between users and the System shall be encrypted using SSL/TLS (HTTPS)

   (b) **Password Security:** All user passwords are stored using industry-standard bcrypt hashing and are never stored in plain text

   (c) **Access Control:** Role-based access control ensuring users can only access features and data appropriate to their role

   (d) **Branch Isolation:** Each school's data is logically separated; no school can access another school's data

   (e) **Server Security:** The hosting server shall be protected with firewalls, intrusion detection, and regular security updates

   (f) **Backup Encryption:** All backup data shall be encrypted at rest

   (g) **Audit Trail:** The System maintains logs of significant user actions for accountability

## ARTICLE 11: TERM AND TERMINATION

11.1 **Term:** This Agreement shall commence on the date of signing and shall continue for the duration of the active subscription, subject to renewal.

11.2 **Termination by The School:**

   (a) The School may terminate this Agreement at any time by providing **thirty (30) days written notice** to DCK Solutions

   (b) No refund shall be provided for unused portions of a monthly subscription period

   (c) For yearly subscriptions cancelled before the end of the year:
   - If cancelled within the first 3 months: **50% refund** of the remaining period
   - If cancelled after 3 months: **No refund**
   - The computer must be returned as per Article 7.7

11.3 **Termination by DCK Solutions:**

   (a) DCK Solutions may terminate this Agreement if The School:
   - Fails to pay subscription fees for more than **ninety (90) days** after the due date
   - Breaches any material term of this Agreement and fails to remedy within **thirty (30) days** of written notice
   - Uses the System for illegal purposes
   - Attempts to reverse engineer, copy, or redistribute the System

   (b) DCK Solutions shall provide **thirty (30) days written notice** before termination (except in cases of illegal use)

11.4 **Upon Termination:**

   (a) All access to the System shall be revoked

   (b) The School's data shall be preserved for **sixty (60) days** after termination

   (c) During the 60-day period, The School may request a data export (Article 5.4)

   (d) After the 60-day period, data shall be permanently deleted unless otherwise agreed in writing

   (e) Any computer provided under Article 7 shall be returned within fourteen (14) days

   (f) All outstanding fees shall become immediately due and payable
